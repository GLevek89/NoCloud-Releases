# NoCloud changelog

A short summary of each published version. The full English and French notes
are on each [release page](https://github.com/GLevek89/NoCloud-Releases/releases).
Every update keeps the existing activation, business profiles, and records.

## 1.9.3 — October 2026

- Invoices accept any client name. A name that is not a saved client yet is
  added to Clients when the invoice is saved; a saved client's name reuses
  that client.
- Clicking an invoice or estimate line description lists every item, and
  searches in invoices, estimates, and Counter Sale show every match.
- Several invoices can be printed at once, and Send offers email, a Desktop
  folder, or an accountant package (PDFs, invoice list, and one zip file).
- Accountant requests: when the accountant asks for a period's invoices,
  NoCloud offers to prepare the package and the email in one step.
- Parts search matches spelling variants such as "ubolt", "u bolt", and
  "1/2x12"; the window fits small laptop screens.
- Version 1.9.2 was prepared but not published; its changes are included here.

[Release notes](https://github.com/GLevek89/NoCloud-Releases/releases/tag/v1.9.3)

## 1.9.1 — August 2026

- Publisher reporting removed from the application; activation stays local.
- Stronger checks against accidentally publishing sensitive values.

[Release notes](https://github.com/GLevek89/NoCloud-Releases/releases/tag/v1.9.1)

## 1.9.0 — August 2026

- Consistent rules for estimate conversion, payments, HST, and stock.
- Safer first start, startup recovery, and restore.
- Accurate rental vacancy and tenant states.
- A dedicated Expense Spreadsheet for entering many receipts.

[Release notes](https://github.com/GLevek89/NoCloud-Releases/releases/tag/v1.9.0)
